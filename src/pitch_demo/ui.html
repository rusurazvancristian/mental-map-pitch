    Jury Challenge — Full-Stack Response
    From Raw Data to
    3D Intelligence
    The jury provided raw video footage from a mobile robot and asked us to build a perception model over the data and present results. From a single RGB camera we built two perception engines — one rebuilds the space as an explorable 3D map offline, the other measures live, real-time distance to people on the edge device itself.
